# ZAYMAX 1.0.2

ZAYMAX 1.0.2 brings the redesigned Dark Liquid Glass interface together with major workout, progress and iPhone integrations.

## Highlights

- Per-set repetitions and weights can be edited before and during a workout.
- Correct workout duration across Lock Screen and background transitions.
- Daily and weekly step totals through optional, read-only Apple Health access.
- Diary profile, BMI overview, training days and Lock Screen note widget.
- Exercise skipping without deleting it from the workout.
- Shareable post-workout ZAYMAX summary image.
- Local JSON backup and restore.
- German, English and Polish.
- Extensive layout, navigation, widget and workout-summary fixes.

Privacy remains central: no account, no advertising, no tracking and no developer-operated storage of workout or Health data.
