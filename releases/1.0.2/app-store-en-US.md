## Description

ZAYMAX is a focused workout app for planning, completing and documenting your own training without unnecessary clutter.

Create custom workouts and set repetitions and weight for every individual set. During training, edit values instantly, check off completed sets, skip an exercise for today and use the built-in rest timer. Workout duration remains accurate when the screen is locked.

After training, ZAYMAX shows a clear summary. Progress and personal bests are easy to understand, and you can share a minimal workout image through the iOS share sheet.

Use the diary for thoughts, training notes and planned workout days. Optional weight, height and birthday details provide local BMI and age information. A selected note can appear in the ZAYMAX iPhone Lock Screen widget.

With your permission, ZAYMAX reads only step-count data from Apple Health and displays daily and weekly totals. It never writes Health data or uploads it to ZAYMAX servers.

Features:
• Create and edit custom workouts
• Repetitions and weights for every set
• Edit values during an active workout
• Complete sets or skip an exercise for today
• Rest timer and reliable workout duration
• History, progress and personal bests
• Record perceived effort after training
• Share a workout-summary image
• Diary, training days, profile and BMI overview
• Steps from Apple Health with your permission
• Lock Screen note widget
• Local JSON backup and restore
• German, English and Polish
• No ZAYMAX account required
• No ads and no subscriptions

Workout, diary and profile data is processed locally on your device. Exports happen only when you start them.

ZAYMAX keeps the focus where it belongs: your workout.
