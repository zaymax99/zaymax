The workout edition has no in-app reporting dialog and sends no automatic error reports. You choose which details to share.

## Workout edition and older Health versions

The revised workout edition focuses on **Today** and **Journal**. It has no Health or Steps tab, requests no Health permission and does not read or write Apple Health data. Manually entered profile values and the local BMI remain available independently.

This change is delivered through an app update. Older App Store or TestFlight versions already installed may still contain the previous Health or Steps features. This guide does not mean your installed version has already received the change.

You can review and revoke previous permissions for ZAYMAX in the Health app or iOS privacy settings. Removing the integration does not delete data from Apple Health.

## Rest timer, sounds and haptics

Under **Settings → Training → Rest timer**, you can turn the rest timer on or off and choose the rest duration. Under **Sounds & haptics**, adjust **App sounds**, the **Sound theme** and **Haptic feedback**; haptics can be switched independently of sounds. Use **Advanced** to turn individual sound events on or off.

For rest-complete sounds while your iPhone is locked, enable **Also while the screen is locked** and allow notifications. If no sound plays, also check App sounds, the **Rest complete** sound event, volume, Silent mode, Focus and **iOS Settings → Notifications → ZAYMAX**. Rest reminders are scheduled locally on the iPhone.

## Lock Screen note widget

Select a note in **Journal** with **Show in Lock Screen widget**. Then press and hold the iPhone Lock Screen, choose **Customize**, open the widget area and add the ZAYMAX note widget. If it is missing, open the app once and restart the iPhone. Remove the Lock Screen selection in ZAYMAX when you no longer want the note in the widget. Avoid confidential text because widget content can be visible on the Lock Screen.

## Backup before reinstalling

Choose **Settings → Save backup** and save the JSON file outside ZAYMAX through the iOS share sheet. Verify the file before deleting or reinstalling the app. To restore, choose **Settings → Load backup**, select your ZAYMAX JSON file and confirm the replacement of your current local data. The backup is not encrypted and may contain personal workout, journal, profile and settings data. Without a backup, the developer cannot recover locally deleted data.

## Delete local data

You can delete individual items in the app. **Settings → Delete all local data** removes the workout, profile, journal and settings data managed by ZAYMAX, along with the selected widget note.

This does not delete backup files already created or shared workout images. A backup copy created in the app's local file area during export can remain even if you cancel sharing. Deleting the app removes that local file area. Files saved outside the app and device or cloud backups must be managed and deleted separately at their storage location or through the relevant provider.

## First troubleshooting steps

- Fully close and reopen ZAYMAX.
- Check the App Store for an update.
- Restart the iPhone.
- For rest reminders, check notification permission, sound settings and device settings.
- On older installations, revoke previous Health permissions in iOS if needed.
